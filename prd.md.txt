# SYNCLOTH — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

# OVERVIEW

Syncloth is a modern Indian D2C streetwear ecommerce platform selling affordable factory-direct fashion products.

Initial categories:
- Oversized Tees
- Jerseys

The platform should feel:
- Fast
- Premium
- Youthful
- Minimal
- Mobile-first

Inspired by:
- The Souled Store

---

# BUSINESS GOALS

Primary Goals:
1. Sell products online
2. Build strong streetwear brand identity
3. Generate wholesale enquiries
4. Build scalable ecommerce platform

---
